[SIZE=5][B]English Version[/B][/SIZE]
Every teleport your players need in one lightweight plugin. UltraTeleports brings TPA requests with clickable answers, a safe random teleport, /back and instant staff teleports. Warm-ups, cooldowns and optional costs keep everything fair.

[B]⭐ Features at a glance:[/B]
[LIST]
[*]📨 [B]TPA Requests:[/B] /tpa and /tpahere with clickable [Accept] and [Deny] buttons and automatic expiry.
[*]🔒 [B]Privacy Controls:[/B] Turn requests off with /tptoggle or ignore single players with /tpignore.
[*]↩️ [B]/back:[/B] Return to your last location or to the place where you died.
[*]🎲 [B]Safe Random Teleport:[/B] /rtp only lands on solid, harmless ground – no lava, water or cactus. Works in the Nether too.
[*]💰 [B]Costs & Cooldowns:[/B] Own cooldown per teleport type and optional /rtp costs via Vault (e.g. UltraEconomy).
[*]🛡️ [B]Staff Teleports:[/B] /tp, /tphere and /tpall – instant, without warm-up.
[*]✨ [B]Clean Layout:[/B] Feedback in the action bar, only errors in the chat – the same look in every Ultra plugin.
[*]🚀 [B]Lightweight:[/B] No required dependencies; runs on Spigot, Paper and Purpur.
[/LIST]

[B]🌍 Languages:[/B] English, Deutsch, Français, Nederlands, Italiano – every player automatically gets the language of their Minecraft client. Change the default with /ultrateleports language <code>.
[B]📦 Requirements:[/B] Spigot, Paper, Purpur · Minecraft 1.20.4 – 26.3 · Java 17+ · no required dependencies · Vault (optional, for /rtp costs)

[SPOILER=Commands & Permissions (EN)]
[LIST]
[*][B]/tpa <player>[/B] – Ask to teleport to a player · [I]ultrateleports.tpa[/I] (default: everyone)
[*][B]/tpahere <player>[/B] – Ask a player to come to you · [I]ultrateleports.tpahere[/I] (default: everyone)
[*][B]/tpaccept[/B] – Accept a request · [I]ultrateleports.tpa[/I] (default: everyone)
[*][B]/tpdeny[/B] – Deny a request · [I]ultrateleports.tpa[/I] (default: everyone)
[*][B]/back[/B] – Return to your last location · [I]ultrateleports.back[/I] (default: everyone)
[*][B]/rtp [world][/B] – Teleport to a random location · [I]ultrateleports.rtp[/I] (default: everyone)
[*][B]/tptoggle[/B] – Turn incoming requests on or off · [I]ultrateleports.toggle[/I] (default: everyone)
[*][B]/tpignore <player>[/B] – Ignore the requests of one player · [I]ultrateleports.toggle[/I] (default: everyone)
[*][B]/tp <player> [target][/B] – Teleport to a player, or one player to another · [I]ultrateleports.staff[/I] (default: OP)
[*][B]/tphere <player>[/B] – Teleport a player to you · [I]ultrateleports.staff[/I] (default: OP)
[*][B]/tpall[/B] – Teleport everyone to you · [I]ultrateleports.staff[/I] (default: OP)
[*][B]/ultrateleports <help|reload|version|language>[/B] – Help, reload, version and language · [I]ultrateleports.admin[/I] (default: OP)
[/LIST]
[B]More permissions:[/B]
[LIST]
[*][I]ultrateleports.bypass.warmup[/I] – Skip the teleport warm-up (default: OP)
[*][I]ultrateleports.bypass.cooldown[/I] – Skip cooldowns (default: OP)
[*][I]ultrateleports.*[/I] – All permissions of the plugin (default: OP)
[/LIST]
[/SPOILER]
[SPOILER=View Full Configuration (EN)]
[CODE]
# Default language: en, de, fr, nl, it
# Change it here or in-game with /ultrateleports language <code>.
language: en

# true: every player gets the plugin in the language of their Minecraft client,
# as long as it is one of the five above. Everyone else gets "language".
use-client-language: true

# Colours used in every message and GUI.
theme:
  primary: "#8B5CF6"
  accent: "#22D3EE"
  muted: "#94A3B8"
  separator: "#64748B"
  error: "#F87171"
  error-text: "#FCA5A5"

sounds:
  enabled: true
  success: {key: "entity.experience_orb.pickup", pitch: 1.4}
  error: {key: "block.note_block.bass", pitch: 0.6}
  tick: {key: "block.note_block.hat", pitch: 1.0}
  teleport: {key: "entity.enderman.teleport", pitch: 1.0}
  click: {key: "ui.button.click", pitch: 1.2}

# Seconds a player has to stand still before a teleport. 0 = instant.
warmup: 3
# Seconds between two teleports of the same kind. 0 = none.
cooldown: 0
# Overrides "cooldown" for single teleport types (tpa, back, rtp).
cooldowns:
  rtp: 60

# Seconds until a teleport request expires.
request-timeout: 60

# /back also returns to the place of death.
back-on-death: true

rtp:
  # Worlds /rtp may be used in.
  worlds: [world]
  # Distance from the centre of the world border, in blocks.
  min-radius: 200
  max-radius: 3000
  max-attempts: 12
  # Price per teleport. Needs Vault and an economy plugin such as UltraEconomy. 0 = free.
  cost: 0
[/CODE]
[/SPOILER]
