## English Version

Every teleport your players need in one lightweight plugin. UltraTeleports brings TPA requests with clickable answers, a safe random teleport, /back and instant staff teleports. Warm-ups, cooldowns and optional costs keep everything fair.

### ⭐ Features at a glance:

- 📨 **TPA Requests:** /tpa and /tpahere with clickable [Accept] and [Deny] buttons and automatic expiry.
- 🔒 **Privacy Controls:** Turn requests off with /tptoggle or ignore single players with /tpignore.
- ↩️ **/back:** Return to your last location or to the place where you died.
- 🎲 **Safe Random Teleport:** /rtp only lands on solid, harmless ground – no lava, water or cactus. Works in the Nether too.
- 💰 **Costs & Cooldowns:** Own cooldown per teleport type and optional /rtp costs via Vault (e.g. UltraEconomy).
- 🛡️ **Staff Teleports:** /tp, /tphere and /tpall – instant, without warm-up.
- ✨ **Clean Layout:** Feedback in the action bar, only errors in the chat – the same look in every Ultra plugin.
- 🚀 **Lightweight:** No required dependencies; runs on Spigot, Paper and Purpur.

**🌍 Languages:** English, Deutsch, Français, Nederlands, Italiano – every player automatically gets the language of their Minecraft client. Change the default with /ultrateleports language <code>.

**📦 Requirements:** Spigot, Paper, Purpur · Minecraft 1.20.4 – 26.3 · Java 17+ · no required dependencies · Vault (optional, for /rtp costs)

<details><summary>Commands & Permissions (EN)</summary>

- `/tpa <player>` – Ask to teleport to a player · `ultrateleports.tpa` (default: everyone)
- `/tpahere <player>` – Ask a player to come to you · `ultrateleports.tpahere` (default: everyone)
- `/tpaccept` – Accept a request · `ultrateleports.tpa` (default: everyone)
- `/tpdeny` – Deny a request · `ultrateleports.tpa` (default: everyone)
- `/back` – Return to your last location · `ultrateleports.back` (default: everyone)
- `/rtp [world]` – Teleport to a random location · `ultrateleports.rtp` (default: everyone)
- `/tptoggle` – Turn incoming requests on or off · `ultrateleports.toggle` (default: everyone)
- `/tpignore <player>` – Ignore the requests of one player · `ultrateleports.toggle` (default: everyone)
- `/tp <player> [target]` – Teleport to a player, or one player to another · `ultrateleports.staff` (default: OP)
- `/tphere <player>` – Teleport a player to you · `ultrateleports.staff` (default: OP)
- `/tpall` – Teleport everyone to you · `ultrateleports.staff` (default: OP)
- `/ultrateleports <help|reload|version|language>` – Help, reload, version and language · `ultrateleports.admin` (default: OP)

**More permissions:**

- `ultrateleports.bypass.warmup` – Skip the teleport warm-up (default: OP)
- `ultrateleports.bypass.cooldown` – Skip cooldowns (default: OP)
- `ultrateleports.*` – All permissions of the plugin (default: OP)

</details>

<details><summary>View Full Configuration (EN)</summary>

```yaml
# Default language: en, de, fr, nl, it
# Change it here or in-game with /ultrateleports language <code>.
language: en

# true: every player gets the plugin in the language of their Minecraft client,
# as long as it is one of the five above. Everyone else gets "language".
use-client-language: true

# Colours used in every message and GUI.
theme:
  primary: "#8B5CF6"
  accent: "#22D3EE"
  muted: "#94A3B8"
  separator: "#64748B"
  error: "#F87171"
  error-text: "#FCA5A5"

sounds:
  enabled: true
  success: {key: "entity.experience_orb.pickup", pitch: 1.4}
  error: {key: "block.note_block.bass", pitch: 0.6}
  tick: {key: "block.note_block.hat", pitch: 1.0}
  teleport: {key: "entity.enderman.teleport", pitch: 1.0}
  click: {key: "ui.button.click", pitch: 1.2}

# Seconds a player has to stand still before a teleport. 0 = instant.
warmup: 3
# Seconds between two teleports of the same kind. 0 = none.
cooldown: 0
# Overrides "cooldown" for single teleport types (tpa, back, rtp).
cooldowns:
  rtp: 60

# Seconds until a teleport request expires.
request-timeout: 60

# /back also returns to the place of death.
back-on-death: true

rtp:
  # Worlds /rtp may be used in.
  worlds: [world]
  # Distance from the centre of the world border, in blocks.
  min-radius: 200
  max-radius: 3000
  max-attempts: 12
  # Price per teleport. Needs Vault and an economy plugin such as UltraEconomy. 0 = free.
  cost: 0
```

## Deutsche Version

Alle Teleports, die deine Spieler brauchen, in einem schlanken Plugin. UltraTeleports bringt TPA-Anfragen mit klickbaren Antworten, einen sicheren Random-Teleport, /back und sofortige Team-Teleports. Warmup, Cooldowns und optionale Kosten sorgen für Fairness.

### ⚙️ Deine Vorteile:

- 📨 **TPA-Anfragen:** /tpa und /tpahere mit klickbaren [Annehmen]- und [Ablehnen]-Buttons und automatischem Ablauf.
- 🔒 **Privatsphäre:** Anfragen mit /tptoggle abschalten oder einzelne Spieler mit /tpignore ignorieren.
- ↩️ **/back:** Zurück zum letzten Ort oder zum Todespunkt.
- 🎲 **Sicherer Random-Teleport:** /rtp landet nur auf festem, ungefährlichem Boden – keine Lava, kein Wasser, kein Kaktus. Auch im Nether.
- 💰 **Kosten & Cooldowns:** Eigener Cooldown pro Teleport-Art und optionale /rtp-Kosten über Vault (z. B. UltraEconomy).
- 🛡️ **Team-Teleports:** /tp, /tphere und /tpall – sofort, ohne Warmup.
- ✨ **Sauberes Layout:** Rückmeldungen in der Actionbar, nur Fehler im Chat – gleicher Look in jedem Ultra-Plugin.
- 🚀 **Leichtgewichtig:** Keine Pflicht-Abhängigkeiten; läuft auf Spigot, Paper und Purpur.

**🌍 Sprachen:** English, Deutsch, Français, Nederlands, Italiano – jeder Spieler bekommt automatisch die Sprache seines Minecraft-Clients. Standardsprache ändern mit /ultrateleports language <code>.

**📦 Voraussetzungen:** Spigot, Paper, Purpur · Minecraft 1.20.4 – 26.3 · Java 17+ · keine Pflicht-Abhängigkeiten · Vault (optional, für /rtp-Kosten)
