# Release-Übersicht

Titel und Taglines aller Plugins für SpigotMC, BuiltByBit, Modrinth und Hangar. Pro Plugin liegt in
`Ultra<Name>/release/` alles zum Hochladen: Beschreibungen, Bilder und eine Checkliste
(`release/README.md`). Jede Seite hat dort einen eigenen Ordner mit `title.txt`, `tagline.txt`
und der Beschreibung.

| Plugin | Titel SpigotMC / BuiltByBit | Name Modrinth / Hangar | Tagline (alle vier Seiten) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [UltraSpawn](UltraSpawn/release) | UltraSpawn | Multi-Spawn & Void Rescue | UltraSpawn | Multiple spawns, first-join spawn and void rescue. Clean action-bar messages in 5 languages. |
| [UltraTeleports](UltraTeleports/release) | UltraTeleports | TPA, RTP & Back | UltraTeleports | TPA requests, safe random teleport and /back with warm-ups. Action-bar messages in 5 languages. |
| [UltraPunish](UltraPunish/release) | UltraPunish | Bans, Mutes & Punish GUI | UltraPunish | Bans, mutes, kicks and warnings with a template GUI and full history. Available in 5 languages. |
| [UltraHomes](UltraHomes/release) | UltraHomes | Homes GUI with Limits | UltraHomes | Set, manage and publish homes in a clean GUI with permission limits. Available in 5 languages. |
| [UltraWarps](UltraWarps/release) | UltraWarps | Warp GUI & Player Warps | UltraWarps | Server and player warps with categories, icons and visit counter in one GUI. 5 languages. |
| [UltraChat](UltraChat/release) | UltraChat | Formats, Channels & Filter | UltraChat | Rank formats, channels, private messages and a spam, caps and ad filter. Available in 5 languages. |
| [UltraEconomy](UltraEconomy/release) | UltraEconomy | Vault Multi-Currency | UltraEconomy | Vault economy with multiple currencies, baltop GUI and transaction log. Available in 5 languages. |
| [UltraKits](UltraKits/release) | UltraKits | Kit GUI, Preview & Editor | UltraKits | Kits with GUI preview, cooldowns, prices and an in-game editor. Available in 5 languages. |
| [UltraVanish](UltraVanish/release) | UltraVanish | Full Vanish for Staff | UltraVanish | True invisibility for staff: tab list, player count, silent chests and vanish levels. 5 languages. |
| [UltraReports](UltraReports/release) | UltraReports | Report GUI & Staff Inbox | UltraReports | Player reports with a reason GUI, staff inbox, Discord webhook and UltraPunish link. 5 languages. |
