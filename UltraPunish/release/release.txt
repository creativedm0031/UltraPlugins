[SIZE=5][B]English Version[/B][/SIZE]
Moderation without the chaos. UltraPunish covers bans, mutes, kicks and warnings – by command or through a GUI with reason templates that escalate automatically. Every punishment is stored in a clear history, and staff are informed in their own language.

[B]⭐ Features at a glance:[/B]
[LIST]
[*]🔨 [B]All Punishments:[/B] Ban, temp-ban, IP ban, mute, temp-mute, kick and warn with durations like 30m, 7d or 1d12h.
[*]📋 [B]Punish GUI & Templates:[/B] Click a reason – each repeat escalates automatically (e.g. Spam: 30m → 2h → 1d).
[*]⚠️ [B]Warning Actions:[/B] Run commands automatically when a player reaches a number of warnings.
[*]📜 [B]Full History:[/B] Every punishment with reason, staff member, date, duration and status in a GUI.
[*]🕵️ [B]Alt Detection:[/B] Staff are alerted when someone joins from the IP of a banned account.
[*]🛡️ [B]Exempt & Notify:[/B] Protect staff with ultrapunish.exempt; notifications reach everyone with ultrapunish.notify.
[*]✨ [B]Clean Layout:[/B] Feedback in the action bar, only errors in the chat – the same look in every Ultra plugin.
[*]🚀 [B]Lightweight:[/B] No required dependencies; runs on Spigot, Paper and Purpur.
[/LIST]

[B]🌍 Languages:[/B] English, Deutsch, Français, Nederlands, Italiano – every player automatically gets the language of their Minecraft client. Change the default with /ultrapunish language <code>.
[B]📦 Requirements:[/B] Spigot, Paper, Purpur · Minecraft 1.20.4 – 26.3 · Java 17+ · no required dependencies

[SPOILER=Commands & Permissions (EN)]
[LIST]
[*][B]/punish <player>[/B] – Open the punish GUI for a player · [I]ultrapunish.gui[/I] (default: OP)
[*][B]/ban <player> [reason][/B] – Ban a player permanently · [I]ultrapunish.ban[/I] (default: OP)
[*][B]/tempban <player> <duration> [reason][/B] – Ban a player for a time · [I]ultrapunish.ban[/I] (default: OP)
[*][B]/banip <player> [reason][/B] – Ban a player and their address · [I]ultrapunish.ban[/I] (default: OP)
[*][B]/mute <player> [reason][/B] – Mute a player permanently · [I]ultrapunish.mute[/I] (default: OP)
[*][B]/tempmute <player> <duration> [reason][/B] – Mute a player for a time · [I]ultrapunish.mute[/I] (default: OP)
[*][B]/kick <player> [reason][/B] – Kick a player · [I]ultrapunish.kick[/I] (default: OP)
[*][B]/warn <player> [reason][/B] – Warn a player · [I]ultrapunish.warn[/I] (default: OP)
[*][B]/unban <player>[/B] – Lift a ban · [I]ultrapunish.revoke[/I] (default: OP)
[*][B]/unmute <player>[/B] – Lift a mute · [I]ultrapunish.revoke[/I] (default: OP)
[*][B]/history <player>[/B] – Show the history of a player · [I]ultrapunish.history[/I] (default: OP)
[*][B]/ultrapunish <help|reload|version|language>[/B] – Help, reload, version and language · [I]ultrapunish.admin[/I] (default: OP)
[/LIST]
[B]More permissions:[/B]
[LIST]
[*][I]ultrapunish.notify[/I] – Receive staff notifications (default: OP)
[*][I]ultrapunish.exempt[/I] – Cannot be punished / reported (default: OP)
[*][I]ultrapunish.*[/I] – All permissions of the plugin (default: OP)
[/LIST]

[SPOILER=View Full Configuration (EN)]
[CODE]
# Default language: en, de, fr, nl, it
# Change it here or in-game with /ultrapunish language <code>.
language: en

# true: every player gets the plugin in the language of their Minecraft client,
# as long as it is one of the five above. Everyone else gets "language".
use-client-language: true

# Colours used in every message and GUI.
theme:
  primary: "#8B5CF6"
  accent: "#22D3EE"
  muted: "#94A3B8"
  separator: "#64748B"
  error: "#F87171"
  error-text: "#FCA5A5"

sounds:
  enabled: true
  success: {key: "entity.experience_orb.pickup", pitch: 1.4}
  error: {key: "block.note_block.bass", pitch: 0.6}
  tick: {key: "block.note_block.hat", pitch: 1.0}
  teleport: {key: "entity.enderman.teleport", pitch: 1.0}
  click: {key: "ui.button.click", pitch: 1.2}

# Commands a muted player cannot use.
muted-commands: [msg, tell, w, r, me, say]

# Tell staff when someone joins from the same address as a banned account.
alt-detection: true

# Console commands that run when a player reaches this number of warnings. %player% = name.
warn-actions:
  3: "tempban %player% 1d Too many warnings"
  5: "ban %player% Too many warnings"

# Buttons of the /punish GUI.
# type: BAN, MUTE, KICK or WARN
# durations: each repeat of the same reason moves one step further; the last step is kept.
#            Use "permanent" for no end. Not used for KICK and WARN.
templates:
  spam:
    icon: PAPER
    type: MUTE
    reason: "Spam"
    durations: [30m, 2h, 1d]
  insult:
    icon: WRITABLE_BOOK
    type: MUTE
    reason: "Insulting"
    durations: [1h, 1d, 7d]
  advertising:
    icon: OAK_SIGN
    type: MUTE
    reason: "Advertising"
    durations: [1d, 7d, permanent]
  behaviour:
    icon: YELLOW_DYE
    type: WARN
    reason: "Behaviour"
  griefing:
    icon: TNT
    type: BAN
    reason: "Griefing"
    durations: [3d, 14d, permanent]
  cheating:
    icon: DIAMOND_SWORD
    type: BAN
    reason: "Cheating"
    durations: [7d, 30d, permanent]
[/CODE]

[*]📜 [B]Komplette Historie:[/B] Jede Strafe mit Grund, Teammitglied, Datum, Dauer und Status in einem GUI.
[*]🕵️ [B]Alt-Erkennung:[/B] Das Team wird gewarnt, wenn jemand von der IP eines gebannten Accounts joint.
[*]🛡️ [B]Schutz & Meldungen:[/B] Team mit ultrapunish.exempt schützen; Meldungen erreichen alle mit ultrapunish.notify.

[/LIST]
