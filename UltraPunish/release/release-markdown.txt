## English Version

Moderation without the chaos. UltraPunish covers bans, mutes, kicks and warnings – by command or through a GUI with reason templates that escalate automatically. Every punishment is stored in a clear history, and staff are informed in their own language.

### ⭐ Features at a glance:

- 🔨 **All Punishments:** Ban, temp-ban, IP ban, mute, temp-mute, kick and warn with durations like 30m, 7d or 1d12h.
- 📋 **Punish GUI & Templates:** Click a reason – each repeat escalates automatically (e.g. Spam: 30m → 2h → 1d).
- ⚠️ **Warning Actions:** Run commands automatically when a player reaches a number of warnings.
- 📜 **Full History:** Every punishment with reason, staff member, date, duration and status in a GUI.
- 🕵️ **Alt Detection:** Staff are alerted when someone joins from the IP of a banned account.
- 🛡️ **Exempt & Notify:** Protect staff with ultrapunish.exempt; notifications reach everyone with ultrapunish.notify.
- ✨ **Clean Layout:** Feedback in the action bar, only errors in the chat – the same look in every Ultra plugin.
- 🚀 **Lightweight:** No required dependencies; runs on Spigot, Paper and Purpur.

**🌍 Languages:** English, Deutsch, Français, Nederlands, Italiano – every player automatically gets the language of their Minecraft client. Change the default with /ultrapunish language <code>.

**📦 Requirements:** Spigot, Paper, Purpur · Minecraft 1.20.4 – 26.3 · Java 17+ · no required dependencies

<details><summary>Commands & Permissions (EN)</summary>

- `/punish <player>` – Open the punish GUI for a player · `ultrapunish.gui` (default: OP)
- `/ban <player> [reason]` – Ban a player permanently · `ultrapunish.ban` (default: OP)
- `/tempban <player> <duration> [reason]` – Ban a player for a time · `ultrapunish.ban` (default: OP)
- `/banip <player> [reason]` – Ban a player and their address · `ultrapunish.ban` (default: OP)
- `/mute <player> [reason]` – Mute a player permanently · `ultrapunish.mute` (default: OP)
- `/tempmute <player> <duration> [reason]` – Mute a player for a time · `ultrapunish.mute` (default: OP)
- `/kick <player> [reason]` – Kick a player · `ultrapunish.kick` (default: OP)
- `/warn <player> [reason]` – Warn a player · `ultrapunish.warn` (default: OP)
- `/unban <player>` – Lift a ban · `ultrapunish.revoke` (default: OP)
- `/unmute <player>` – Lift a mute · `ultrapunish.revoke` (default: OP)
- `/history <player>` – Show the history of a player · `ultrapunish.history` (default: OP)
- `/ultrapunish <help|reload|version|language>` – Help, reload, version and language · `ultrapunish.admin` (default: OP)

**More permissions:**

- `ultrapunish.notify` – Receive staff notifications (default: OP)
- `ultrapunish.exempt` – Cannot be punished / reported (default: OP)
- `ultrapunish.*` – All permissions of the plugin (default: OP)

</details>

<details><summary>View Full Configuration (EN)</summary>

```yaml
# Default language: en, de, fr, nl, it
# Change it here or in-game with /ultrapunish language <code>.
language: en

# true: every player gets the plugin in the language of their Minecraft client,
# as long as it is one of the five above. Everyone else gets "language".
use-client-language: true

# Colours used in every message and GUI.
theme:
  primary: "#8B5CF6"
  accent: "#22D3EE"
  muted: "#94A3B8"
  separator: "#64748B"
  error: "#F87171"
  error-text: "#FCA5A5"

sounds:
  enabled: true
  success: {key: "entity.experience_orb.pickup", pitch: 1.4}
  error: {key: "block.note_block.bass", pitch: 0.6}
  tick: {key: "block.note_block.hat", pitch: 1.0}
  teleport: {key: "entity.enderman.teleport", pitch: 1.0}
  click: {key: "ui.button.click", pitch: 1.2}

# Commands a muted player cannot use.
muted-commands: [msg, tell, w, r, me, say]

# Tell staff when someone joins from the same address as a banned account.
alt-detection: true

# Console commands that run when a player reaches this number of warnings. %player% = name.
warn-actions:
  3: "tempban %player% 1d Too many warnings"
  5: "ban %player% Too many warnings"

# Buttons of the /punish GUI.
# type: BAN, MUTE, KICK or WARN
# durations: each repeat of the same reason moves one step further; the last step is kept.
#            Use "permanent" for no end. Not used for KICK and WARN.
templates:
  spam:
    icon: PAPER
    type: MUTE
    reason: "Spam"
    durations: [30m, 2h, 1d]
  insult:
    icon: WRITABLE_BOOK
    type: MUTE
    reason: "Insulting"
    durations: [1h, 1d, 7d]
  advertising:
    icon: OAK_SIGN
    type: MUTE
    reason: "Advertising"
    durations: [1d, 7d, permanent]
  behaviour:
    icon: YELLOW_DYE
    type: WARN
    reason: "Behaviour"
  griefing:
    icon: TNT
    type: BAN
    reason: "Griefing"
    durations: [3d, 14d, permanent]
  cheating:
    icon: DIAMOND_SWORD
    type: BAN
    reason: "Cheating"
    durations: [7d, 30d, permanent]
```

## Deutsche Version

Moderation ohne Chaos. UltraPunish deckt Bans, Mutes, Kicks und Verwarnungen ab – per Befehl oder über ein GUI mit Grund-Vorlagen, die automatisch eskalieren. Jede Strafe landet in einer übersichtlichen Historie, und das Team wird in seiner eigenen Sprache informiert.

### ⚙️ Deine Vorteile:

- 🔨 **Alle Strafen:** Ban, Tempban, IP-Ban, Mute, Tempmute, Kick und Warn mit Dauern wie 30m, 7d oder 1d12h.
- 📋 **Punish-GUI & Vorlagen:** Grund anklicken – jede Wiederholung eskaliert automatisch (z. B. Spam: 30m → 2h → 1d).
- ⚠️ **Verwarnungs-Aktionen:** Befehle laufen automatisch, sobald ein Spieler eine bestimmte Anzahl Verwarnungen erreicht.
- 📜 **Komplette Historie:** Jede Strafe mit Grund, Teammitglied, Datum, Dauer und Status in einem GUI.
- 🕵️ **Alt-Erkennung:** Das Team wird gewarnt, wenn jemand von der IP eines gebannten Accounts joint.
- 🛡️ **Schutz & Meldungen:** Team mit ultrapunish.exempt schützen; Meldungen erreichen alle mit ultrapunish.notify.
- ✨ **Sauberes Layout:** Rückmeldungen in der Actionbar, nur Fehler im Chat – gleicher Look in jedem Ultra-Plugin.
- 🚀 **Leichtgewichtig:** Keine Pflicht-Abhängigkeiten; läuft auf Spigot, Paper und Purpur.

**🌍 Sprachen:** English, Deutsch, Français, Nederlands, Italiano – jeder Spieler bekommt automatisch die Sprache seines Minecraft-Clients. Standardsprache ändern mit /ultrapunish language <code>.

**📦 Voraussetzungen:** Spigot, Paper, Purpur · Minecraft 1.20.4 – 26.3 · Java 17+ · keine Pflicht-Abhängigkeiten
