[SIZE=5][B]English Version[/B][/SIZE]
Give your server a spawn system that just works. UltraSpawn manages one or many spawn points, sends new players to their own first-join spawn and catches everyone who falls into the void. Every message appears cleanly in the action bar – in the language of each player.

[B]⭐ Features at a glance:[/B]
[LIST]
[*]📍 [B]Multiple Spawns:[/B] Main spawn plus as many named spawns as you like, each with exact position and view direction.
[*]👋 [B]First-Join Spawn:[/B] New players start at their own spawn – perfect for a tutorial or rules area.
[*]👑 [B]Rank & World Spawns:[/B] VIP spawn via permission (ultraspawn.group.<name>) and optional spawns per world.
[*]🔄 [B]Join & Respawn:[/B] Teleport on join and on respawn, each toggleable – beds and respawn anchors can be respected.
[*]🕳️ [B]Void Rescue:[/B] Players falling out of the world are caught and placed back at spawn instead of dying.
[*]⏱️ [B]Warm-up & Cooldown:[/B] Countdown in the action bar, cancelled on movement or damage; bypass permissions for staff.
[*]✨ [B]Clean Layout:[/B] Feedback in the action bar, only errors in the chat – the same look in every Ultra plugin.
[*]🚀 [B]Lightweight:[/B] No required dependencies; runs on Spigot, Paper and Purpur.
[/LIST]

[B]🌍 Languages:[/B] English, Deutsch, Français, Nederlands, Italiano – every player automatically gets the language of their Minecraft client. Change the default with /ultraspawn language <code>.
[B]📦 Requirements:[/B] Spigot, Paper, Purpur · Minecraft 1.20.4 – 26.3 · Java 17+ · no required dependencies

[SPOILER=Commands & Permissions (EN)]
[LIST]
[*][B]/spawn [player][/B] – Teleport to spawn · [I]ultraspawn.spawn[/I] (default: everyone)
[*][B]/setspawn [name][/B] – Set a spawn point (default, firstjoin, a world or a rank) · [I]ultraspawn.set[/I] (default: OP)
[*][B]/delspawn <name>[/B] – Delete a spawn point · [I]ultraspawn.delete[/I] (default: OP)
[*][B]/ultraspawn <help|reload|version|language>[/B] – Help, reload, version and language · [I]ultraspawn.admin[/I] (default: OP)
[/LIST]
[B]More permissions:[/B]
[LIST]
[*][I]ultraspawn.spawn.others[/I] – Send other players to spawn (default: OP)
[*][I]ultraspawn.bypass.warmup[/I] – Skip the teleport warm-up (default: OP)
[*][I]ultraspawn.bypass.cooldown[/I] – Skip cooldowns (default: OP)
[*][I]ultraspawn.*[/I] – All permissions of the plugin (default: OP)
[*][I]ultraspawn.group.<name>[/I] – Spawn at the spawn <name> (e.g. a VIP spawn)
[/LIST]
[/SPOILER]
[SPOILER=View Full Configuration (EN)]
[CODE]
# Default language: en, de, fr, nl, it
# Change it here or in-game with /ultraspawn language <code>.
language: en

# true: every player gets the plugin in the language of their Minecraft client,
# as long as it is one of the five above. Everyone else gets "language".
use-client-language: true

# Colours used in every message and GUI.
theme:
  primary: "#8B5CF6"
  accent: "#22D3EE"
  muted: "#94A3B8"
  separator: "#64748B"
  error: "#F87171"
  error-text: "#FCA5A5"

sounds:
  enabled: true
  success: {key: "entity.experience_orb.pickup", pitch: 1.4}
  error: {key: "block.note_block.bass", pitch: 0.6}
  tick: {key: "block.note_block.hat", pitch: 1.0}
  teleport: {key: "entity.enderman.teleport", pitch: 1.0}
  click: {key: "ui.button.click", pitch: 1.2}

# Seconds a player has to stand still before /spawn teleports. 0 = instant.
warmup: 3
# Seconds between two uses of /spawn. 0 = none.
cooldown: 0

teleport-on-join: false
teleport-on-respawn: true
# true: players with a bed or respawn anchor respawn there instead.
respect-bed-spawn: true

# true: a spawn named like a world (e.g. /setspawn world_nether) is used inside that world.
per-world-spawns: false

# Players falling out of the world are sent back to spawn instead of dying.
void-rescue:
  enabled: true
  # Blocks below the bottom of the world.
  depth: 5
  # Empty = all worlds.
  worlds: []
[/CODE]
[/SPOILER]

[SIZE=5][B]Deutsche Version[/B][/SIZE]
Gib deinem Server ein Spawn-System, das einfach funktioniert. UltraSpawn verwaltet einen oder mehrere Spawnpunkte, schickt neue Spieler an ihren eigenen First-Join-Spawn und fängt jeden auf, der ins Void fällt. Alle Meldungen erscheinen sauber in der Actionbar – in der Sprache jedes Spielers.

[B]⚙️ Deine Vorteile:[/B]
[LIST]
[*]📍 [B]Mehrere Spawns:[/B] Hauptspawn plus beliebig viele benannte Spawns, jeweils mit exakter Position und Blickrichtung.
[*]👋 [B]First-Join-Spawn:[/B] Neue Spieler starten an einem eigenen Spawn – ideal für Tutorial oder Regelbereich.
[*]👑 [B]Rang- & Welt-Spawns:[/B] VIP-Spawn per Permission (ultraspawn.group.<name>) und optional eigene Spawns pro Welt.
[*]🔄 [B]Join & Respawn:[/B] Teleport beim Betreten und beim Respawn, einzeln schaltbar – Betten und Seelenanker werden auf Wunsch beachtet.
[*]🕳️ [B]Void-Rettung:[/B] Wer aus der Welt fällt, wird aufgefangen und zurück zum Spawn gesetzt, statt zu sterben.
[*]⏱️ [B]Warmup & Cooldown:[/B] Countdown in der Actionbar, Abbruch bei Bewegung oder Schaden; Bypass-Permissions fürs Team.
[*]✨ [B]Sauberes Layout:[/B] Rückmeldungen in der Actionbar, nur Fehler im Chat – gleicher Look in jedem Ultra-Plugin.
[*]🚀 [B]Leichtgewichtig:[/B] Keine Pflicht-Abhängigkeiten; läuft auf Spigot, Paper und Purpur.
[/LIST]

[B]🌍 Sprachen:[/B] English, Deutsch, Français, Nederlands, Italiano – jeder Spieler bekommt automatisch die Sprache seines Minecraft-Clients. Standardsprache ändern mit /ultraspawn language <code>.
[B]📦 Voraussetzungen:[/B] Spigot, Paper, Purpur · Minecraft 1.20.4 – 26.3 · Java 17+ · keine Pflicht-Abhängigkeiten
